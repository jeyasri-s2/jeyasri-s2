About Me

I’m Jeyasri, a passionate Engineer with a robust background in programming and research, specializing in Software Engineering and AI/ML. With 15 years of experience in the IT industry, I’ve successfully delivered over 10 production-ready projects. I’ve had the opportunity to work across all three major cloud platforms—AWS, GCP, and MS Azure. Throughout my career, I’ve led teams to success by making key design decisions, mentoring junior developers, hosting weekly reading groups, and organizing pair programming sessions. Additionally, I’ve conducted onsite data collection experiments and prepared training-ready datasets from custom data, as well as developed synthetic video data using the Unity game engine. I began my career as a Java programmer and later expanded into mobile application development for both Android and iOS. In 2014, I developed cross-platform applications using HTML5, CSS3, and jQuery. Currently, I’m working as an Applied Scientist, leading AI/ML research projects that involve dataset preparation, data cleaning, programming with PyTorch and TensorFlow, deploying pipelines on AWS and Azure, and conducting performance evaluations.
