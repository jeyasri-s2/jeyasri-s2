01 / About

About

I'm a Staff Machine Learning Engineer with 15+ years scaling production AI/ML systems — spanning generative AI and LLMs, computer vision, and NLP. My work sits at the point where research becomes a shipped feature: distributed GPU training, multi-cloud MLOps, and the engineering discipline to get a model past a notebook and in front of users.

I've led cross-functional teams across offshore and onsite locations, taken products from architecture decisions through deployment, and hold two granted US patents alongside a published computer-vision paper at ICCV. Recent work includes a real-time FastAPI migration for financial decisioning at USAA, and — over eight years at SRI International — everything from CNN-based occlusion detection to reinforcement-learning models for detecting fraudulent trading activity.

I started my career in Chennai, India, and spent the next 12 years onshore in the US — delivering production systems in person across HCL America, SRI International, and SR Systems, working directly alongside US-based teams and stakeholders throughout. I've since returned to India and am now looking for my next role here, bringing that global delivery experience with me.
